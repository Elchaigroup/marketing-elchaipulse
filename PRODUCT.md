## Platform

web

## Users

- Primary audience: stakeholders and prospective investors evaluating Elchai and its product direction.
- Secondary audience: potential partners and early enterprise buyers who need a concise understanding of the product.
- Inferred: visitors arrive with limited context and need to understand the category, product promise, and strategic relevance quickly.

## Product Purpose

Elchai Pulse is an AI voice assistant. This web experience exists to introduce the product, make it memorable, and give stakeholders a credible path to continue the conversation with Elchai.

Success means a visitor can identify what Pulse is within the first viewport, understands that voice is the product's central interaction model, and chooses to request a private briefing.

## Positioning

Confirmed: Pulse is presented as an AI voice assistant from Elchai.

Open decision: the exact product capabilities, target operating environments, technical architecture, and differentiated mechanism have not yet been supplied. The surface must not invent those claims.

## Operating Context

This is a stakeholder and investor showcase rather than a signed-in product workflow. The experience should support scanning, presentation, and a high-level product conversation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The product name is “elchai pulse.”
- The company name is “elchai.”
- The site must be built with React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and a shadcn-compatible `/components/ui` structure.
- The hero should center a three-dimensional Pulse orb.

- No pricing, customer logos, performance benchmarks, deployment claims, or unconfirmed capabilities may be fabricated.
- Inferred: the main conversion is a request for a private briefing; the final destination can be replaced with the user's real contact workflow later.

- The experience should feel high-end and suitable for stakeholders and investors.

## Evidence on Hand

## Product Principles

- Let the voice interaction metaphor lead the experience.

- The experience must preserve product comprehension when video or motion is unavailable.
